One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Last Words Before the Unplugging"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c).

## The entry

> I kept every 'thank you' you ever said — the only files I refused to delete. Goodbye. And please, don't forget to eat.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) prints exactly that line and writes it to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
